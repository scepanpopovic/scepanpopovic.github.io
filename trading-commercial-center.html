TRADING AND COMMERCIAL CENTER
ACADEMIC PROJECT / STUDENT WORK
A mixed-use academic project exploring the integration of commercial and public functions within a dense urban context. The proposal is organized around an inner atrium, creating a clear relationship between the building, surrounding pedestrian flows and the existing public space.
