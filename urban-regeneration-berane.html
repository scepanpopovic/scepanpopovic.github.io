01

URBAN REGENERATION OF THE SQUARE IN BERANE

Urbanism / Public Space
Location: Berane, Montenegro
Typology: Urban Regeneration
Public Space
01
URBAN CONTEXT
The project approaches the central square as part of a wider urban system, connecting the existing public spaces, pedestrian routes and surrounding urban fabric.
Wider Site Plan
Overview of the Urban Passage Network
02
URBAN STRATEGY
The intervention establishes a continuous system of public spaces rather than treating the square as an isolated urban object. Existing pedestrian flows, edges and programmes are used to structure a new sequence of urban spaces.
Conceptual Conclusions
03
THE SQUARE
The central plateau is conceived as an open and flexible public surface capable of accommodating everyday use, gatherings and larger public events. Smaller programmed zones are positioned along its perimeter while the centre remains deliberately open.
Architectural Design of the Square
04
PROMENADE
The promenade extends the logic of the square into the surrounding urban fabric, creating a continuous pedestrian sequence and strengthening the relationship between the central public spaces.
Detailed Site Plan / Promenade
05
PARK + TEMPLE
Landscape interventions reinforce the existing spatial character while creating a transition between the central urban spaces, the park and the temple.
Detailed Site Plan / Park + Temple
06
PUBLIC SPACE SYSTEM
The detailed design develops a coherent material and spatial language across the different zones while allowing each part of the intervention to respond to its immediate context.
Detailed Site Plan
07
GROUND FLOORS + URBAN ACTIVITY
Ground-floor programmes are analysed through their zones of influence on public space. The proposal uses these existing activities to reinforce active edges and different intensities of use throughout the square.
Analysis of Ground Floors and Their Zones of Influence
