Landscapes of Memory is a research project developed during a research residency at the Faculty of Architecture, Czech Technical University in Prague.

The project explores the relationship between architecture, literature, landscape and collective memory, examining socialist and post-socialist space through architectural research, landscape theory and artistic practice.

The publication forms part of the wider Landscapes of Memory project and brings together spatial research, archival material, writing and visual documentation.
