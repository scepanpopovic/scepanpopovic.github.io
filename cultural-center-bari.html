03 / ARCHITECTURE
CULTURAL CENTER + NURSERY + KINDERGARTEN
ACADEMIC PROJECT / 2024
An academic project developed in Bari exploring the integration of cultural and educational functions within a shared architectural complex. The proposal combines a cultural center, nursery and kindergarten, organizing different programs around a system of collective, transitional and outdoor spaces.
