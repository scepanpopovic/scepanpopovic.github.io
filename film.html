LANDSCAPES OF MEMORY

Landscapes of Memory is a video essay developed during a research residency at the Faculty of Architecture, ČVUT in Prague. Through film, spatial narration, archival material, and sound composition, the work explores landscape as a space of memory, ideological transformation, and historical continuity within socialist and post-socialist contexts.

This project is supported by UNESCO through the Culture and Creativity for the Western Balkans (CC4WBs) project, funded by the European Union. The project focuses on fostering dialogue in the Western Balkans by enhancing the cultural and creative sectors for increased socio-economic impact.
